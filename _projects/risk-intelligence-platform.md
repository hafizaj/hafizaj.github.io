## Context

Risk data at the central bank lived in 52 separate manual risk profiles, each with its own format, owner, and update cadence. Building an institution-wide risk picture meant chasing 52 departments by hand, and it was out of date the moment it was assembled.

## What I built

- Digitised all 52 manual risk profiles into a single **centralised SharePoint risk register**, replacing spreadsheets and email chains with one structured, always-current source
- Built **Power Automate workflows** around the update cycle so departments submit and refresh their risk data without manual chasing or reformatting
- Fed the register into a **live Power BI dashboard**, giving management real-time cross-departmental visibility instead of a static quarterly snapshot

## Results

- **5,000 hours** of analyst effort eliminated annually
- A single authoritative, always-current risk view where there had been 52 disconnected, manually maintained ones
- Meaningful productivity gains across the risk function from digitising a process that used to run almost entirely on manual collection and reformatting
